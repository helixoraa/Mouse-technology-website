Our Products
What our products do.
Two products in development for everyday transport and payments, with more technology products planned.
In development
Cashless Bus Fare Platform
A simpler way to pay for the bus.
Passengers pay their bus fare with a supported transit card. The system connects the card with a balance record, processes the fare when the passenger taps, and provides transaction information for passengers and bus operators.
What we are going to develop
Passenger cards and balances
Register or link a supported transit card and keep its balance and transaction records.
Fare payments
Process the agreed fare when a card is presented to a supported reader, with a clear payment outcome.
Balance top-ups
Add balance through the supported top-up process.
Passenger information
Secure access to your own balance and transaction history.
Operator tools
Manage route and vehicle information, review fare transactions and check daily revenue and reconciliation records.

Payment / Money Transfer Platform
A clear and convenient way to send money.
A digital payment and money-transfer product that helps users send money through a straightforward process, with the transfer steps, transaction information and progress easy to understand.
Planned capabilities
User accounts
Sign up and manage your details.
Recipient details
Add who the money is going to.
Transfer amount entry
Enter how much you want to send.
Review of details and charges
See the transaction details and applicable charges before you confirm.
Confirmation, status and history
Confirm the transfer, follow its status and revisit past transactions.
